# Coverage

What DogeLib implements, per operation and per kind of evidence ([CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#coverage-and-status)).
Each column records a separate claim.
A blank cell means nothing exists.
Each operation links to its full per-area entry under `docs/coverage/`, which holds complete theorem hypotheses, fixture counts, and exclusions.

## Legend

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Definition | Executable definition in the specification target, or proof/tool module when noted |
| Theorems | Named theorems or links to their hypotheses and boundaries |
| Comparison | Finite comparison evidence: known answers, corpora, and Core comparisons |
| Refinement | Proved relationship to another implementation, naming that implementation |
| Excluded | Key inputs, modes, or eras outside the current claims |

Use *conditional* in the Theorems column when a theorem depends on a hypothesis that no caller yet discharges.
A word in italics after a theorem name, such as *property* or *witness*, is the tag that the theorem has in the Lean source ([`theorem_tag`](ARCHITECTURE.md#layout)).
A theorem name with no such word has no tag, which says nothing about its kind.

## Proposed revisions

This section records proposed revisions and the framework that states them ([design record 0007](docs/design/0007-proposed-revisions.md)).
A proposed revision is not part of the baseline, and it never gets a row in the table of [operations](#operations).
Its columns have the same meaning, with three labels for the level of a compatibility theorem:

- *Script-local*: a theorem about one Script evaluation or one input, through `evalScript`, `verifyScript` or `verifyLegacySpend`.
- *Header-local*: a theorem about one header check.
- *Block-level*: a theorem about `validateBlock` or `connectBlock` for one block on a supplied coin set and chain view, or about a list of blocks through `connectChain`. The revised side is `connectBlockWith` or `validateBlockWith` ([design record 0011](docs/design/0011-mainnet-ranges.md), section 10.1, and [design record 0012](docs/design/0012-relative-lock-time.md), section 8).

A Script-local or header-local soft-fork theorem is not a soft-fork claim about the network.
That claim also needs block validity, a block-level activation and a legacy projection (design record 0007, section 8.3).
A block-level theorem is not a claim about the network either.
It holds for mainnet rules, for a supplied view and coin set, and inside the domains of design record 0010, section 3.
It says nothing about fork choice, convergence or liveness.
Two theorems are about a list of blocks, for connection alone: `connectChainWith_restricts` and `relativeLockTime_chain`.

The tables below separate the framework, public proposals, and the two test-only framework examples.
Neither example is a proposal.
`RelativeLockTime` states rules that discussion 3509 of `dogecoin/dogecoin` proposed for Dogecoin (SOURCES.md, P4).
It has the three rules of design record 0012: BIP 113, BIP 112 and BIP 68.
DogeLib takes no position on the activation of these rules.

One kind of evidence exists for `RelativeLockTime` alone, and only this section and [docs/coverage/proposals.md](docs/coverage/proposals.md) record it.
Dogecoin Core holds the code of the three rules, and their version-bits state never becomes `ACTIVE` on mainnet.
The Core harness can seed that state, so that Core's own code of a rule runs (design record 0012, section 10.2).
Each entry for that evidence starts with the words "Core at `e0a1c15` with the CSV state forced to `ACTIVE`", and ends with the sentence about mainnet.
It is not a Core comparison of the baseline, and no row of the table of operations cites it.

## Campaign infrastructure

`scripts/campaign.py` implements stages 1 to 4 of [design record 0009](docs/design/0009-bug-finding-campaign.md#13-stages-and-exit-conditions) for the 2-of-3 multisig-with-recovery template, signed by `core-signer --sign`.
It checks properties Q1 to Q7, separates six outcome classes, saves failures, reduces plans (`campaign.py reduce`), and tests the planted fault through `proposal-replay`.

At seed `20261108`, the default campaign compares 1,897 requests with zero in all six outcome classes.
The generated campaign completes 3,000 cases (9,408 assessed Q6 requests) with zero disagreements or process failures.
The planted proposal search finds 16 three-instruction violations in the first 5,840 faulty-rule cases, and zero across all 374,480 corrected-rule cases.
See [docs/campaign.md](docs/campaign.md#recorded-campaign-results) for full counts, self-test mutant detections M1 to M6, reduction results, and commands.
